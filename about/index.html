Background

My name is Savvas Chanlaridis (pronounced Sáv-vas Han-la-ri-dis). I earned my BSc in Physics from the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, where I minored in Nuclear and Elementary Particle Physics. I then moved to Germany to pursue an MSc in Astrophysics at the University of Bonn.

In 2020, I returned to Aristotle University as a guest researcher in the Gravitational Waves group led by Nikolaos Stergioulas. There, I studied the effect of stellar winds on the evolution of low-mass helium stars. From 2021 to 2024, I conducted my doctoral research at the University of Crete, where I worked in the Stellar AfterLife & Transients group at the Institute of Astrophysics - FORTH under the supervision of John Antoniadis, focusing on numerical modelling of the formation and evolution of neutron stars.

In 2024, I began a second MSc in Data Science and Machine Learning at the Hellenic Open University, completing the degree in 2026. My interest in data-driven methods also led me to move into industry in 2025.

I joined Kivos Analytics as a Data Scientist, working on LLM-based applications and statistical evaluation methods. In March 2026, I joined EY as a Senior Consultant in Technology Consulting, where I work on applied AI and machine learning solutions.
