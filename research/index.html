Doctoral Work

Numerical modeling of the formation and evolution of neutron stars.

My PhD, awarded by the University of Crete in 2024, brought together numerical studies of supernova progenitors, binary stellar evolution, and compact-star formation. It examined the relationship between stellar physics, explosion pathways, and the resulting properties of neutron stars.

The dissertation is accessible via the Greek National Archive of PhD Theses. Its research forms a foundation for my broader interest in computational approaches to complex physical systems.
